## Credit deduction happens up front

When `POST /api/v1/generate` is accepted, credits are deducted immediately, before the job enters the queue. If the job ends `failed`, the credits are refunded automatically. You do not pay for engine failures.

What is **not** refunded: jobs you cancel yourself after they were successfully dispatched, and requests rejected before a job was created (so those never deducted anything).

## How the price is calculated

The server looks up the price in the following order and stops at the first match:

<Steps>
  <Step title="`quality:resolution`">
    For image models that have both keys, for example `low:2K`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="`resolution:duration`">
    Combinations such as `720p:15`; the check tolerates order and the trailing `s`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="`duration`">
    A single override per duration, for example `"30": 15`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="`resolution`">
    A single override per resolution, for example `"4K": 2`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="`creditsDefault`">
    The model's base price.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That is why **prices can differ for requests that look the same**: `resolution: "720p", duration: "30"` can cost more than `duration: "15"`.

<Info>
  Price numbers are set by admins and can change. The only source of truth is `priceMatrix` and `creditsDefault` from `GET /api/v1/models` — do not copy a price table into your code.
</Info>

## Reference cost

Adding a reference image or video does not increase the price today (`referenceSurcharge` is 0). For motion control models, the reference video's duration actually determines the output duration — and that duration is what gets billed.

## Not enough credits

```json theme={null}
{
  "error": "Kredit akun tidak mencukupi. Pembuatan ini membutuhkan 10 kredit",
  "requiredCredits": 10,
  "currentCredits": 4
}
```

Status `402`. No job is created and no credits are used. The cheapest way to avoid this error is to read your balance before sending a batch — see [`GET /api/v1/user/credits`](/en/api-reference/credits).

## Unlimited packages

An Unlimited purchase grants a time-based quota, not a credit balance. While it is active, generation on **eligible models** deducts 0 credits and the `creditsCharged` field in the response is `0`.

There are limits:

- Only certain video/image models are covered by the Unlimited quota. Other models still deduct normal credits even on an Unlimited account. Check eligibility with `unlimited.active` from `/api/v1/user/credits`, then match models through the dashboard.
- The plan's flagship model is `gemini-omni-1.2-unlimited`. Its Gemini sparkle watermark is removed automatically before the job turns `succeeded`, with no extra credit charge and no special parameter.
- Maximum of **2 concurrent processes**. A third is answered `429`.
- The daily video quota still applies (50 successful videos per day).

## Pay and check your balance

Top up credits through the [dashboard](https://vidgen.id/billing) (QRIS). Balance monitoring should happen on your server, not in the frontend:

```bash theme={null}
curl https://vidgen.id/api/v1/user/credits \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer vg_KEY_KAMU"
```

```json theme={null}
{
  "success": true,
  "user": { "uid": "...", "email": "...", "credits": 128 },
  "unlimited": { "active": false, "daysLeft": 0, "expiresAt": null },
  "limits": {
    "maxConcurrentJobs": 2,
    "currentActiveJobs": 0,
    "dailyVideoLimit": 50,
    "dailyVideosGenerated": 12,
    "dailyVideosRemaining": 38,
    "resetsAt": "00:00 WIB"
  }
}
```

<Warning>
  `credits` can be a fractional number (for example 1.5) because some image models cost less than one credit. Do not round down on your side before comparing it with a price.
</Warning>
